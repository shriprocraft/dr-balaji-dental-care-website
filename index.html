Everyday Orthodontics

A Pathway to Mastery in Orthodontic Practice

About the Program

A 6-month, module-based training program for general dentists and dental professionals who want to build real orthodontic skill for their everyday practice — led by Dr. Balaji Jagan.

Duration

6 months

Frequency

Two classes per month

Format

Bi-monthly classes with hands-on workshops

Course Highlights

What you'll gain

Practical Insights

Real-world scenarios drawn from the intricacies of everyday orthodontic practice.

Patient-Centric Approach

Communication strategies and patient management techniques that improve the patient experience.

Cutting-Edge Techniques

The latest clinical techniques and emerging trends in orthodontics.

Hands-On Workshops

Theoretical knowledge applied in a simulated clinical environment.

Key Features

How the program runs

  • Comprehensive, month-by-month modules that build a strong foundation and progressively advance your expertise
  • Instruction from experienced professionals sharing practical, real-world insight
  • Hands-on exercises, case studies, and collaborative case reports
  • A bi-monthly class structure that balances learning with time to absorb and apply it

Why Attend

Built for practising dentists

  • A carefully curated curriculum covering essential aspects of everyday orthodontic practice
  • Networking opportunities with like-minded dental professionals
  • Continued professional development aligned with the latest advancements in the field
